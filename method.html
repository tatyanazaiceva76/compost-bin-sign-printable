Method, privacy and limits

Back to the sign tool

What it does

The tool places user-entered bin titles and accepted/excluded lines into one measured sign per PDF page. Copy order follows the project order. Preview and PDF are generated from the same layout model.

What it does not do

It does not classify materials, provide municipal or collector rules, copy official logos, or promise environmental, compliance or contamination outcomes. Verify every rule with your own collector or operator before confirming the project.

Layout refusal

The tool refuses unsupported glyphs, invalid dimensions, more than 8 bins, more than 32 pages, and text that cannot fit without clipping. It does not silently truncate or shrink content into unreadable text.

Privacy

Project processing and PDF generation happen in the browser. The page loads packaged static assets from the same origin and makes no third-party runtime requests. It sends no project data to third parties and uses no uploads, accounts, analytics, cookies or persistent storage. Project JSON and PDF downloads remain under the user's control. Normal hosting access logs are outside this browser-local tool and are not represented here.

Third-party components

PDF generation uses pdf-lib 1.17.1 and @pdf-lib/fontkit 1.1.1, self-hosted in this package. The pdf-lib notice is retained in pdf-lib-LICENSE.md. Fontkit's published MIT metadata, author attribution and a clearly labelled standard licence-text reference are recorded in fontkit-LICENSE-REFERENCE.md; its retained package supplies no standalone upstream notice, so no copyright holder or year is inferred. Text is embedded with Noto Sans Regular from notofonts/noto-fonts commit ffebf8c1ee449e544955a7e813c54f9b73848eac, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 in OFL-1.1.txt. No official sorting artwork or brand assets are included.
